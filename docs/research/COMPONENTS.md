# Component research — SCOTT Scale 980 Dark Grey (L)

What each part looks like and its key measurements (for the 3D model), plus useful facts (for the app).
✅ official (SCOTT / Shimano / Suntour) · 🟡 dealer or secondary source · 📐 estimate (to measure from photos) · 🔍 check on the bike.
`slot` = the part's id in the app and the node name in the 3D model.

## Frame & fork

### `frame` — Scale Alloy 6061
- ✅ 6061 custom-butted aluminium, hydroformed tubes, internal cable routing, replaceable derailleur hanger, Boost QR 5×141 mm rear, tapered head tube, BB92 press-fit (41 × 89.5 mm shell).
- ✅ Geometry and 3D coordinates: see [BIKE-SPECS.md](../BIKE-SPECS.md).
- **Look:** matte dark grey. Big neon-yellow "SCOTT" letters with a black outline on the down tube. Gloss-black "SCOTT" on the top tube near the head tube. Visible weld beads at the head tube junction. Oval cable-entry ports on both sides of the down tube near the head tube. Black protector on the drive-side chainstay. Bottle bosses on the seat tube and down tube. (Official studio photo.)
- 📐 Tube sizes (not published, estimate from photo and fit): head tube tapered ~56 mm top / ~66 mm bottom OD (to hold 50/62 mm cups); down tube ~55–60 mm, wide and slightly flattened; top tube ~40–45 mm, flattened oval; seat tube ~35 mm (31.6 post + wall); chainstays ~20–25 mm; seatstays ~16–18 mm.
- 🟡 Derailleur hanger: SCOTT part **#254094** (Boost 141 QR alloy Scale). 🔍 Confirm before buying.
- 🟡 Seat clamp 34.9 mm. 🔍 QR or bolt.

### `fork` — Suntour XCR32 RL-R (coil)
- ✅ 100 mm travel, coil spring, rebound adjust, **remote lockout** (cable to a bar remote), **1 1/8" straight steerer** (runs in a tapered head tube via the reducer headset).
- ✅ Suntour XCR family: 32 mm stanchions (black, Ni-plated/hard-anodised), magnesium lowers, forged aluminium crown, **post mount 160 mm direct** (so the 180 mm front rotor uses a +20 mm adapter), 9 × 100 mm QR dropout, from ~2.45 kg.
- 🟡 Axle-to-crown **501 mm** (dealer data from SCOTT). Agrees with the official geometry (503 mm including the headset cup). Suntour lists 517–518 mm for the newer tapered XCR, so don't use that for this fork.
- 🟡 Offset: Suntour lists 46 mm; SCOTT's geometry implies 51 mm. 🔍 Not critical for the model.
- **Look:** all black (crown, stanchions, lowers), remote cable entering the top of the right leg.
- Service: wipe stanchions after every ride; lower-leg service every ~50 h; full service every ~100 h.
- ⚠️ Some 2022 builds may have a **Suntour X1 32 RL-R tapered** instead. 🔍 Read the decal.

### `headset` — Syncros OE Press Fit (reducer)
- ✅ Tapered 1.5" → 1 1/8" reducer; cups OD 50/62 mm, bearing ID 44/56 mm.
- Because of the reducer, a tapered-steerer fork can be fitted later with a different lower cup. That's a good upgrade path.

## Cockpit

### `handlebar` — Syncros Alloy 6061 flat
- ✅ T-shape flat, **740 mm**, 9° backsweep, 31.8 mm clamp. Black, "Syncros" graphics.

### `stem` — Syncros Alloy 6061
- ✅ 31.8 mm clamp, 1 1/8" steerer, 6° rise, 2-bolt faceplate, black with "SYNCROS" on top. Length not published (🔍 printed on the stem; expected 70–80 mm).
- Spacers under the stem visible in the official photo (count them 🔍).

### `grips` — Syncros (neon yellow)
- ✅ (photo) Neon-yellow grips with the Syncros logo on the end. Model not listed in the spec 🔍.

### `shifter` — Shimano Deore SL-M6100-R
- ✅ Right-hand 12-speed Rapidfire Plus trigger, band clamp, cable. Shift cable and housing run inside the frame.
- Also on the bar: **Suntour remote** lockout lever (left side, under the bar).

## Brakes

### `brake_front` / `brake_rear` — Shimano MT200
- ✅ Hydraulic disc, 2-piston post-mount calipers, mineral oil, BL-MT200 levers (black).
- 🟡 Resin pads, B01S-type shape (check the pad box when replacing 🔍).
- Rear caliper sits on the non-drive side of the rear triangle (not visible in the drive-side photo). Mount type 🔍.

### `rotor_front` / `rotor_rear` — Shimano SM-RT10
- ✅ Center Lock, **180 mm front / 160 mm rear**, resin pads only.
- 🟡 New thickness ~1.8 mm; replace at **1.5 mm**.
- **Look:** silver steel rotor with a silver Center Lock lockring.

## Drivetrain

### `crankset` — Shimano FC-MT511-1, 32T
- ✅ (Shimano) 2-piece Hollowtech-II-style 24 mm spindle, **4-arm spider, 96 mm BCD**, **52 mm chainline** (Boost), **Q-factor 178.5 mm**, arm lengths 165/170/175 (🔍 likely 175 on L), Dynamic Chain Engagement+ narrow-wide ring, black.
- **Look:** black arms, black 32T narrow-wide ring with silver bolts.

### `bottom_bracket` — Shimano BB-MT500-PA
- ✅ Press-fit BB92 (41 × 89.5 mm shell), for 24 mm spindles. Needs a bearing press to replace.

### `chain` — KMC X12
- ✅ 12-speed, 1/2" (12.7 mm) pitch, quick link. **Silver** (official photo).
- Replace at **0.5 %** wear (12-speed).

### `cassette` — Shimano Deore CS-M6100-12
- ✅ 12-speed **10-12-14-16-18-21-24-28-33-39-45-51T**, Micro Spline, Hyperglide+. Silver.
- 🟡 Weight figures vary between sources (~460–600 g).
- 3D: largest cog 51T has a ~210 mm outer diameter; smallest 10T ~45 mm.

### `derailleur` — Shimano Deore RD-M6100 SGS
- ✅ 12-speed, long cage (SGS), Shadow RD+ clutch, max cog 51T, total capacity 41T, **13T pulleys**. Black and grey with a "DEORE" logo.

### `pedals` — Femin/Feimin FP873ZU
- ✅ Flat platform pedals (SCOTT's photos and weight exclude pedals). Looks: 🔍 photos needed.

## Wheels & tyres

### `wheel_front` — Syncros rim + Formula CL-51
- ✅ 29" (622 mm BSD) Syncros rim 32H, **black stainless spokes 15G/1.8 mm** (silver nipples in the photo), Formula CL-51 Center Lock hub, QR 9×100.
- 🟡 Rims reported as **not tubeless-compatible** (used-bike listing). 🔍 Check the rim label.

### `wheel_rear` — Syncros rim + Shimano FH-MT401-B
- ✅ Syncros rim 32H, same black spokes, Shimano FH-MT401-B Center Lock hub, **Boost QR 5×141 mm**, Micro Spline freehub.
- 📐 Lacing likely 3-cross 🔍.

### `tire_front` / `tire_rear` — Maxxis Rekon Race 29 × 2.25"
- ✅ 60 TPI, Maxxis logo on the sidewall, black wall.
- 🟡 Wire-bead version ~680–790 g; outer diameter ~736–740 mm.
- **Look:** low, closely spaced ramped centre knobs, taller shoulder knobs. Fast-rolling XC tread.

## Saddle & seatpost

### `seatpost` — Syncros 31.6 × 400 mm
- ✅ Aluminium, black, 31.6 mm diameter, 400 mm long. Rigid (no dropper).

### `saddle` — Syncros Belcarra Regular 2.5
- ✅ Model. 🟡 The closely related Belcarra Regular 2.0 is ~250 × 130 mm with 7 mm CrMo rails; the 2.5 is probably very similar 📐.
- **Look:** black, slim XC shape.
