## Photo checklist

Save into the matching folder in `photos/`. Daylight or even light, no harsh sun, bike upright with wheels straight, plain background if possible.

**`photos/full-bike/`** — from ~3 m away, slight zoom, camera at axle height, square to the bike, tape measure in the shot:
- 🔲 Drive side (chain side), exactly side-on
- 🔲 Non-drive side, exactly side-on
- 🔲 Front, straight on
- 🔲 Rear, straight on
- 🔲 Top-down (if possible)
- 🔲 Three-quarter: front-left, front-right, rear-left, rear-right

**`photos/closeups/`**:
- 🔲 `cockpit/`: bar, stem, grips, shifter, lockout remote, brake levers (from above and from the side)
- 🔲 `frame-and-decals/`: every logo/decal (down tube both sides, top tube, seat tube, head tube, chainstay)
- 🔲 `fork/`: crown, legs, decals, lockout
- 🔲 `drivetrain/`: crank and chainring (both sides), cassette, derailleur, chain, pedals
- 🔲 `brakes/`: front and rear calipers, rotors, hoses
- 🔲 `wheels-and-tyres/`: hubs, rims, tyre sidewall logo, tread
- 🔲 `saddle-and-seatpost/`: saddle top and side, seatpost, clamp
- 🔲 `damage-and-custom/`: scratches, stickers, anything non-stock
